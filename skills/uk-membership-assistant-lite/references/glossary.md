# UK membership and AI glossary (short form)

Use these meanings, and explain terms in plain English when the user seems unfamiliar.

- **AMS / membership CRM**: software holding member records, renewals, payments, events and often CPD.
- **Arrears**: subscriptions due but unpaid, usually within the grace period.
- **CASC**: Community Amateur Sports Club, a UK tax status with its own rules on fees and Gift Aid.
- **Charity Commission**: regulator for charities in England and Wales (OSCR in Scotland, CCNI in Northern Ireland).
- **Lapse rate (churn)**: share of members due to renew who didn't. UK membership teams usually say "lapsed" rather than "churned".
- **Cohort**: members who joined in the same period, tracked together.
- **Company limited by guarantee**: common legal form for UK membership bodies.
- **CPD**: continuing professional development, often required by professional bodies.
- **Data controller / processor**: the organisation deciding how data is used / a supplier handling it on its behalf.
- **Direct Debit**: UK bank payment method for subscriptions.
- **First-year retention**: share of new members who renew at their first renewal.
- **Gift Aid**: UK tax relief for charities on donations; strict rules for membership fees.
- **Grace period**: time after expiry when a member keeps access while payment is chased.
- **Grade / category / tier**: a membership level with its own fee and rights (student, associate, member, fellow).
- **Lapsed member**: someone whose membership expired without renewal.
- **Legitimate interests**: UK GDPR lawful basis for expected uses such as renewal reminders.
- **Membership year**: fixed (everyone renews on one date) or rolling (anniversary of joining).
- **Net growth**: joiners minus leavers.
- **Non-subscription income**: income other than subscriptions: events, training, sponsorship, advertising, publications.
- **Onboarding**: welcoming new members and helping them get value, usually the first 90 days.
- **PECR**: UK rules on electronic marketing; decide when consent is needed.
- **Post-nominals**: letters after a member's name showing their grade.
- **Privy Council**: grants Royal Charters and approves changes to chartered bodies' charters and byelaws.
- **Renewal rate**: members who renewed ÷ members due to renew.
- **Subscription (sub)**: the UK term for a membership fee.
- **UK GDPR**: UK data protection law, with the Data Protection Act 2018; regulator is the ICO.
- **Value proposition**: why someone should join and stay, in terms of outcomes.
- **Win-back**: a campaign to bring lapsed members back.

## AI terms
- **Anonymise**: remove details identifying a person before using data with AI.
- **Hallucination**: an AI stating something false with confidence.
- **Human in the loop**: a person checks and approves AI output before use.
- **Prompt**: the instruction typed into an AI tool.
- **Training data**: text a model learned from; some tools may train on user input unless switched off.
