# Renewal email pattern

Short paragraphs, under about 150 words each, one action per email, plain subject lines, UK English. Merge fields in [square brackets].

- **8 weeks before**: what they've had this year (events attended, resources used if known), what's coming next year, renewal date.
- **4 weeks before**: renew link, Direct Debit option, who to contact.
- **1 week before**: short reminder, one link.
- **On expiry**: "Your membership has ended. It's easy to renew", with the grace period date.
- **2 weeks after**: personal email from a named person: "Did we get something wrong?"

Stop the sequence as soon as someone renews or sets up Direct Debit, and send a short thank-you instead. Fewer, kinder emails retain more members than repeated chasing.
